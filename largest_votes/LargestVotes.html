The Evolution of Mass Voting Through History

From Ancient Athens to Modern Democracies: The Largest Votes Ever Recorded

● Democratic Elections ● Controlled/One-Party Elections ● Mixed/Restricted Franchise

📈 The Record Holder

India's 2019 General Election holds the all-time record with 603.7 million voters - more than the entire population of most continents!

🏛️ Ancient Democracy

Athens (471 BCE) pioneered mass voting with ~8,500 citizens voting on ostracism - revolutionary for its time when most were ruled by kings.

🌍 Geographic Giants

The top 5 largest votes all come from just 4 countries: India (3), Indonesia (1), and historically the USSR (1).

📊 Democratic Progress

From 1848's 7.5 million French voters to 2019's 603.7 million Indian voters - an 80-fold increase in democratic participation!

🗳️ Single-Day Record

Indonesia's 2019 election saw 154 million people vote in just 10 hours - the largest single-day democratic exercise ever.

🚀 Exponential Growth

The 20th century saw voter numbers explode from millions to hundreds of millions as universal suffrage spread globally.
